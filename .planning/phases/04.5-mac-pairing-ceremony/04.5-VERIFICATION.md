1. TLS termination for the `mac_ci` hosted Phoenix endpoint on port 4010 (self-signed cert or equivalent is sufficient — this is a loopback-only, ephemeral CI config already, per its own file header warning).
2. One genuine, recorded execution of `PairingCeremonyTests` against that hosted server, confirmed green.
3. Promotion of `PairingCeremonyTests` to `--required-test` in `run-mac-verification.sh`, and only then flipping `PROT-01` to complete in `REQUIREMENTS.md`.
