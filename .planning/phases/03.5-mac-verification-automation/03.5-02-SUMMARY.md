## Decisions Made

- Kept all four plans serial and explicitly disjoint so later plans can extend a named ownership boundary rather than filter one monolithic target at runtime.
- Made the result bundle authoritative: console exit status participates in aggregation, but named discovery/execution/skip/outcome comes only from `xcresulttool` JSON.
- Used allowlist staging rather than trying to scrub a raw xcresult or DerivedData tree; forbidden material never enters the upload directory.

- **Files modified:** `playstead-mac/Playstead/App/PlaysteadApp.swift`, `playstead-mac/PlaysteadUITests/HostedRunnerCanaryTests.swift`, `playstead-mac/scripts/ci/tests/four-layer-topology-test.sh`
- **Verification:** The guarded full run completed all four layers without another prompt; topology verifies disposable/scoped Keychain and ad-hoc-only signing boundaries.
- **Committed in:** `f5292bd`

- **Files modified:** `playstead-mac/TestPlans/Rendering.xctestplan`
