## Warnings

### WR-02: WARNING — Private-media CAS publication is not atomic with its manifest transaction (CR-02 residual)

**File:** `playstead-server/lib/playstead/metadata/private_media.ex:113-129`; `playstead-server/lib/playstead/blobs/store/local_disk.ex:260-276`
**Issue:** The earlier deterministic leak is fixed: capacity and changeset checks, plus manifest insertion, now precede CAS writes. However, CAS publication is still a filesystem rename followed by a blob-row insert, while the private-media manifest belongs to an outer database transaction. A crash, later CAS/storage failure, or outer transaction failure after bytes are published can leave canonical bytes with no committed manifest/reference. Since committed CAS bytes may be shared and the adapter has no reference-aware deletion/collector, the orphan cannot be safely reclaimed. The fix record explicitly leaves this cross-domain crash boundary unresolved; it is inherited from the shared storage publication contract, not evidence that the rejected-item-limit exploit remains.
**Fix:** Add durable staged publication/recovery or a reference-aware collector that serializes publication with cross-domain references and can prove an object is unreferenced before deletion.
