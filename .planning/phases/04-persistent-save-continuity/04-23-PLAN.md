## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-04-23-01 | Tampering | replayed resolution intents on the wire | high | mitigate | Every send carries the entry-anchored `Idempotency-Key` `SaveOutbox.enqueue` already generates (`"{kind}:{entryID}"`), so a replay of one entry is idempotent server-side while a distinct later intent gets its own key. A test asserts the header value and that a retry replays the same key. |
| T-04-23-02 | Tampering | CAS commit of crash-found bytes | high | mitigate | `CASManager.commit(partialAt:sha256:)` is content-addressed and verifies the digest; the digest is the one the poller computed over the bytes, never recomputed at commit.
