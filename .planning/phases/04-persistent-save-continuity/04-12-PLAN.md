<objective>
Make the only-copy danger case visible at exactly the two moments that matter — when the system cannot fix it itself, and when the user is about to destroy it — and at no other moment.

Purpose: A revision that exists on exactly one device is the most dangerous state in the product. It is also, most of the time, the product working correctly: an offline queue is success, not failure. Tiering by what the user is about to do rather than by how long a state has been true is what lets the product be honest without being alarmist.

Output: A persistent inline escalation panel for the unfixable cases, and an interruptive modal at the moment of destructive intent whose default action is the escape hatch.
</objective>
