<objective>
Wire the capture-and-upload half of persistent save continuity into the shipped app.

Purpose: 04-18's reachability baseline found that `SaveCapturePoller`, `SaveSessionRecovery` and `SaveUploadLane` are never constructed in production. The app can RESTORE a save at launch (04-14 wired that) but nothing in the shipped binary ever captures a save during play, promotes it at session end, replays an abandoned session, or uploads anything. SAVE-01 and SAVE-02 are, as shipped, unmet.

Output: One `SaveSessionCoordinator` owning the per-play capture lifecycle, called from the real Play path; recovery replay at app launch; a live upload lane whose real failure classification feeds the D-40 escalation surface; and an allowlist shrunk by exactly those symbols.
</objective>

<task id="1" title="SaveSessionCoordinator: the per-play capture lifecycle, in one testable type">
<action>
Create `playstead-mac/Playstead/Saves/SaveSessionCoordinator.swift`.

It owns exactly one play session's capture lifecycle and nothing else:

- `begin(saveLineID:targetURL:destinationDirectory:artifactRelativePath:)` — reads the line's current head digest from `SaveStore` (`fetchHead(saveLineID:)?.blobSHA256`), constructs a `SaveCapturePoller` over a `FileSaveArtifactSource` on `targetURL` seeded with that `knownHeadDigest`, calls `startSession()` (D-04's baseline check), persists any baseline capture it returns as a revision, then calls `start()`.
- `end()` — calls `settle()` then `promote()` (D-05/D-04, unconditionally and in that order), persists the promoted capture as a `SaveRevisionRow` with `durability: .localOnly` and `captureMethod: "session"`, parented to the head read at `begin`, and calls `stop()`.

Persisting a capture must go through `SaveStore.insertRevision`, mirroring the row construction `SaveSessionRecovery.replay` already performs (read it and match its field-by-field choices; differ only where the difference is real, i.e. `captureMethod`).

`end()` must be safe to call when `begin` never ran or already ended, and must never throw out to its caller — a capture failure must not surface as "Launch failed". Record failures via the existing `SaveCaptureBlockedState` (currently also unwired) so a blocked capture is visible rather than silent.
</action>
<acceptance>
- `SaveSessionCoordinator.swift` exists and constructs `SaveCapturePoller`, `FileSaveArtifactSource` and `SaveCaptureBlockedState`.
- `PlaysteadTests/SaveSessionCoordinatorTests.swift` covers: baseline capture on an out-of-band change; a promoted revision landing in `SaveStore` after write-then-end; `end()` without `begin()` being a no-op; and a capture failure not propagating.
- No settle/promote/quiescence logic is reimplemented — the coordinator only calls poller methods.
</acceptance>
</task>
